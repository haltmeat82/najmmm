For Najm.

Some apologies arrive late. That doesn’t make the hurt they come from any less real.

N

Najm,

I don’t know if you will read this, and I know I have no right to expect you to.

I’ve spent a long time thinking about what happened between us and about the things I did to you. I cheated on you. I broke your trust, and I hurt someone who had given me a place in her life that I should have respected.

I’m sorry.

Not just because I lost you, not because things ended, and not because I wish the story had gone differently. I’m sorry because you didn’t deserve what I did to you. You deserved honesty, loyalty, and the kind of love that makes you feel safe, not the kind that leaves you questioning whether you were ever enough.

You were enough, Najm. My choices were my responsibility. Nothing about you justified what I did, and I won’t make excuses for it.

I did love you. Whatever that love meant to me, I failed to honour it through my actions. And I understand that saying I loved you doesn’t undo the pain I caused you.

I know an apology cannot return the time you spent with me, erase the memories that hurt, or rebuild trust simply because I wish it could. I cannot ask you to forget. I cannot demand forgiveness. And I cannot expect you to see me differently just because I finally understand some of what I should have understood back then.

I wish I had been a better man when I had the chance to be one for you.

You don’t owe me a reply, a second chance, or even a moment of your time. If silence is what you want from me, I will respect that.

I just wanted these words to exist somewhere, without excuses and without asking anything from you.

I’m genuinely sorry for hurting you, Najm.

I hope life gives you the peace, happiness, and love you always deserved.

Take care of yourself.

— Someone who wishes he had treated your heart more carefully.
